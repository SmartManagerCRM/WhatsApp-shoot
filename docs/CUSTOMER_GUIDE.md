# Screenshot Assistant - Customer guide

**What it does:** when you open a chat in WhatsApp (or WhatsApp Business), the app sends your newest **photo** to that chat for you - no tapping.
Videos and other files are never sent. Everything stays on your phone: the app has no internet access and never reads your messages.

## 1. Install (once)
1. Open the file **ScreenshotAssistant-release.apk** that the seller sent you.
2. If Android asks, allow "Install unknown apps" for the app you opened the file from (WhatsApp / Files). Tap **Install**.
3. If Google Play Protect shows a warning, tap **More details > Install anyway**. This appears because the app is not distributed through the Play Store.
4. Open **Screenshot Assistant**.

## 2. Activate your license (once)
1. The seller sends you your **license** - a long code starting with `WSS1.` (or a license file).
2. Open the app, go to the **License** tab, **paste** the code (or tap **From file** and pick the file) and tap **Activate**.
   The tab then shows **License active** - that is all.

## 3. Set it up (about 2 minutes)
Open the **Home** tab. The checklist shows what is still needed - tap the button next to each item:
- **Accessibility service** - tap *Turn on*, find **Screenshot Assistant** (under Accessibility > Installed apps) and switch it on. This is how the app notices an open chat and presses WhatsApp's own buttons. It never reads message text.
- **Photo access** and **Video access (dates only)** - tap *Allow* and choose **Allow all**. Video access only lets the app check that your newest item is a photo, not a video; videos are never opened.
- **Notifications** - tap *Allow* (shows the "automation is on" indicator).

Then open **Settings**:
- **What to send:** *Newest photo* (default).
- **When to send:** *Once per chat* (sends once to each chat; use *Reset this chat* on Home to send again), or *Every chat open*.
- **Fully automatic sending:** switch **Send automatically** ON and confirm. Without it, the app only prepares the picture and you press Send yourself.

## 4. Use it
1. On **Home**, tap **Start automation** (the card turns green: "Automation is on").
2. Open WhatsApp and open any chat. Wait about 10-15 seconds.
3. The app opens the attach menu and Gallery, picks your newest photo and sends it. A new image appears in the chat.
- **Pause / Stop:** use the buttons on Home. A small **Stop** button also appears over WhatsApp.
- It never sends a video. If it cannot be sure the newest item is a photo, it sends nothing and tells you why on Home.

## 5. If something does not work
1. Make sure Home shows no orange warnings (license, accessibility, photo/video access).
2. Open a chat and wait 15 seconds.
3. Go to **Home > Activity & diagnostics > Copy diagnostics** and send the text to the seller. It contains technical steps only - never message text, names or numbers.

## 6. Good to know
- **WhatsApp updates** can change its screens. If the app stops working after an update, tell the seller; an update of this app may be needed.
- **New phone, reinstall or cleared app data:** ask the seller for your license again and enter it as in step 2. Normal app updates keep your license.
- **Subscriptions:** if your license has an end date, the License tab shows it. Before it ends the seller sends a **renewal code**: paste it under *License > Enter a code*. After the end date the app stops until you renew.
- **Privacy:** no internet permission, no account, no analytics. Pictures are copied only temporarily inside the app and removed afterwards.
- This app drives WhatsApp's own screens. WhatsApp's terms restrict automation of the service; use it for your own business needs and at your own responsibility.
